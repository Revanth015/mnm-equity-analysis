02 / COMPANY & BUSINESS MODEL

Built around movement.
Spread across industries.

Founded in 1945, Mahindra & Mahindra Limited is an operating company and a holding company. Consolidated accounts include subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures.

The operating heart is utility vehicles and farm equipment. The wider group adds financial services, technology, logistics, real estate, hospitality, renewables and industrial businesses.

Auto brings scale and SUV mix; Farm adds a large tractor franchise with seasonal demand; services bring a different earnings rhythm. Group revenue is not a pure automaker measure.

06 / VALUATION & INVESTMENT OUTLOOK

The opportunity is real.
So is the execution bar.

A framework for evaluation, not a buy or sell call. Strong reported results should be tested against earnings durability and cash conversion.

01

What can keep growth going

  • SUV launches, richer mix and EV adoption.
  • Tractor demand, rural incomes and replacement cycles.
  • Services and technology growth levers.
02

What could interrupt it

  • Competition and launch execution.
  • Commodity costs, rates and rural demand.
  • Financial-services asset quality and funding costs.
03

What to monitor next

  • Auto and Farm PBIT margins alongside PAT.
  • Cash generation and borrowing mix.
  • Market share, EV mix and price paid for growth.

Mahindra enters FY27 with strong momentum. The investment case depends on converting it into durable returns and cash flow.
