# Mahindra & Mahindra — Equity Research

**Live site:** https://Revanth015.github.io/mnm-equity-analysis/  
**Research snapshot:** Market price dated 9 November 2026; latest quarter Q2 FY27

An opinionated, source-linked long-form equity research article for Indian investors and finance students. The article presents an Accumulate view, an illustrative valuation range and scenario assumptions, operating drivers, risks, and links to an interactive dashboard.

## Files

- `index.html` — publishable research blog article.
- `dashboard.html` — independent interactive dashboard with investor inference.
- `blog-post.md` — article in Markdown, including the requested verification checklist.
- `methodology.md` — definitions, calculations and comparison limitations.
- `research/sources.md` — source register for cited company, market and peer information.
- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` — GitHub Pages deployment workflow.

Open the live site or open `index.html` locally. The explorer loads Chart.js and fonts from the internet. Historical market observations are snapshots, not live data. The article contains an educational research disclaimer.
