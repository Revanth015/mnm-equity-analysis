# Methodology

Research snapshot: price and market capitalization at the NSE close on 9 November 2026; latest quarter ended 30 September 2026. M&M FY21–FY26 financial figures are consolidated and in ₹ crore unless the article says otherwise. FY21 PAT is continuing-operations basis and is not strictly comparable with later years.

PAT margin = PAT after NCI / income from operations. FY21–FY26 revenue CAGR uses five annual intervals. Trailing PAT is FY26 PAT + H1 FY27 PAT − H1 FY26 PAT; trailing P/E is market capitalization divided by trailing PAT. Dividend yield is recommended FY26 dividend per share divided by the stated share price. Figures are rounded for readability.

The five-year share price return is split/bonus adjusted and excludes dividends. The peer discussion uses each company’s reported FY26 scope and is contextual; it is not a like-for-like profitability ranking. M&M consolidated borrowings include Financial Services funding, so group borrowings and operating cash flow are not industrial-only measures. Segment PBIT margins and peer valuation multiples are cited from external sources or identified as verification items in the article.

Scenario EPS growth and P/E assumptions are illustrative analyst assumptions, not company guidance. Fair value is calculated as scenario EPS multiplied by the selected P/E; scenario outputs are rounded. The Accumulate view is a 12-month opinion based on the stated snapshot and can change with new results or price movement.

Market data and financial information are historical snapshots. The article is educational research and not personalized investment advice.
